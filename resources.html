Resources

Here are some resources, mainly books, that I like.

\((1-\epsilon)\) Cryptography

  • “A Computational Introduction to Number Theory and Algebra” by Victor Shoup
  • “A Course in Cryptography” by Rafael Pass and Abhi Shelat
  • “A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography” by Dan Boneh and Victor Shoup
  • “Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach” by Boaz Barak and Sanjeev Arora
  • “Foundations of Cryptography - Volume 1 (Basic Tools)” by Oded Goldreich
  • “Foundations of Cryptography - Volume 2 (Basic Applications)” by Oded Goldreich
  • “Introduction to Modern Cryptography” by Jonathan Katz and Yehuda Lindell

Other

  • “Algorithms” by Robert Sedgewick and Kevin Wayne
  • “Algorithms Unlocked” by Thomas Cormen
  • “Graph Theory” by Reinhard Diestel
  • “Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces” by Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau and Remzi Arpaci-Dusseau
